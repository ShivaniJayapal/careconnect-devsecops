About CareConnect

CareConnect is a fictional patient appointment portal built as a college mini-project on secure DevOps (DevSecOps) in a healthcare setting.

The goal is not the medical content. The goal is to show how a small web application is tested, security scanned, packaged into a container, deployed to Kubernetes and monitored automatically.

What this demo is

  • A Flask application with validated forms, CSRF protection and secure headers.
  • A local dataset of fictional doctors.
  • A pipeline: GitHub, Jenkins, tests, Bandit, Docker, Trivy, Kubernetes, Prometheus and Grafana.

What this demo is not

  • It is not a production medical records system.
  • It has no user accounts, encryption at rest, audit logging or regulatory compliance work.
  • Never enter real names, emails or health information.
